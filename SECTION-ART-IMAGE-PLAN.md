# Section art - image plan

Two background bands in the same rough flat / scribbly register as the blog
funnel art. Reference points: DigitalOcean's old flat spot illustrations and
Google's flat vector work, not stock and not 3D.

### Image 1 - CTA band, dark (ctaband-dark)
**Role:** ctaband-dark
**Source:** generate
**Filename:** cta-band-dark.webp
**Alt:** flat illustration of Central Valley storefronts with a signal arc rising from one
**Aspect:** 21:9

```
A wide flat vector illustration on a solid near-black background, drawn like a
screenprint with visible grain and slightly misregistered ink.

The subject: a low row of simple Central Valley main-street buildings running
along the bottom edge of a very wide frame, drawn as flat solid silhouettes with
hard edges. Small square windows are punched out of them. From one single
building near the left, three concentric arcs rise and spread outward like a
broadcast signal. From one building near the right, a second smaller set of arcs
rises. Loose hand-drawn scribble marks and scratchy crosshatching overprint a few
of the building faces, as if sketched with a blunt crayon over the flat ink.
A scattering of small dots and short dashes drifts through the upper corners.

Composition: extremely wide and letterboxed. All of the buildings and marks sit
along the bottom edge and in the far left and far right corners. The entire
middle of the frame is empty near-black space with nothing in it, because large
white headline text will be placed there.

Style: flat solid fills, hard edges, no gradients, heavy grain and halftone
speckle, screenprint texture, confident and graphic.

Palette: solid near-black background (hex #111111), a loud burnt orange (hex
#E8590C) for the signal arcs and a few building faces, and warm off-white (hex
#F3F4F6) for the remaining silhouettes and the scattered dots. Only these three
colors.

Intended use: a decorative background band behind the closing call to action on a
web design agency website.

No text, no letters, no numbers, no words anywhere in the image. No beige, no
cream, no purple or blue or lavender AI palette. No people, no cars, no glowing
neon, no lens flare, no 3D render, no glassmorphism, no photorealism, no
watermark.
```

### Image 2 - Fresno band, light (fresnoband-light)
**Source:** generate
**Role:** fresnoband-light
**Filename:** fresno-band-light.webp
**Alt:** faint line illustration of Central Valley orchard rows and the Sierra foothills
**Aspect:** 21:9

```
A wide, extremely faint line illustration on a plain white background, drawn like
a light pencil sketch reproduced on newsprint.

The subject: rows of orchard trees in one-point perspective receding toward a low
horizon across a very wide frame, drawn as simple repeated round shapes on thin
trunks in outline only. Behind them, the low rolling profile of the Sierra Nevada
foothills as a single continuous thin line. To one side, a simple small-town
water tower in outline, and a straight irrigation canal line running from the
foreground toward the horizon. Everything drawn in thin, slightly wobbly
hand-drawn strokes.

Composition: very wide and letterboxed, the horizon sitting low, the whole scene
calm and evenly spread with generous empty white space above it.

Style: outline only with almost no fill, thin uneven hand-drawn line weight,
light paper grain, the whole image extremely low contrast and pale, as if printed
at ten percent opacity.

Palette: white background (hex #FFFFFF) with all linework in a very light warm
grey (hex #E5E7EB). No black, no strong darks anywhere, and no orange.

Intended use: a barely perceptible decorative background behind a service-area
section on a Fresno web design agency website, sitting underneath body text.

No text, no letters, no numbers, no words anywhere in the image. No beige, no
cream, no purple or blue or lavender AI palette. No people, no vehicles, no 3D
render, no photorealism, no heavy shadows, no watermark.
```
